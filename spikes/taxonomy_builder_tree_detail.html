Tree on the left, detail on the right

Click a topic or subtopic in the tree. Selecting a subtopic also puts your cursor in its name field on the right. Keyboard: ↑ ↓ move, ← collapse or go to parent, → expand or go to first child, Del remove, / search, Ctrl+Z undo. Hover a row for its ✕. The toast after a removal counts down for 10 s and holds while you hover it; the header Undo keeps the last 20 removals for as long as the page is open. Search matches names and descriptions; a topic that matches keeps all its subtopics, otherwise only matching ones show. Names update in the tree as you type. An amber dot means a topic has no subtopics yet (the backend requires one). Simulate rejection shows how a backend error lands on the exact row it names.
