How it behaves, and the bits still open

  • The topic button and the row buttons are the same queue. Pressing Generate 12 on Loops opens the topic and ticks all five of its gaps — you can then untick the ones you don't want. Nothing is hidden from you by using the shortcut.
  • Gaps only by default. A full topic's drawer would otherwise be twelve rows of "Full", which is noise. Everything switches to the complete picture when you want to check a subtopic that is already covered.
  • A topic with nothing missing would show a green Fully covered badge instead of a Generate button. No topic in this fake data qualifies — worth deciding whether those topics stay in the table or collapse to a single line at the bottom.
  • "Questions" per topic counts slots, not rows. One question can claim up to three subtopics, so the per-topic numbers add up to more than the 118 in the bank. The header shows the honest distinct count; the column shows what the engine would find.
  • What Generate actually does is the real open question. The generator picks its own subtopic from a textbook chunk you choose — it can't be told to write for "while loops · Hard". So this button honestly means "find chunks that teach these and generate from them, then check what came back". That's a feature to design, not a click handler.
