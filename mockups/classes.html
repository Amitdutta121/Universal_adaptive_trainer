What a student sees when joining

The join page is phone-first and asks for three things. Nothing else is collected.

  1. 1. Open the link or enter the code
  2. 2. Name and emailThe email is how you identify them in the roster and CSV, and how a returning student resumes on a new device.
  3. 3. PractiseQuestions are picked from the class's set, weighted towards the student's weakest subtopics.
  4. Not on the list?With the restriction on, they see "This class is limited to enrolled students. Ask your instructor to add your email."
