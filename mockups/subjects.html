How a tool is cleared

A tool, or a new version of one, is offered to courses only after these pass. Questions stay pinned to the version that checked them.

  1. Self-test. A known-answer probe on every start-up and for each new version. A failure stops new questions using the tool; questions it already checked keep their result.
  2. Published exercises. Sets of 30 end-of-chapter exercises with answer keys from open textbooks (OpenStax, OpenIntro). The tool works them out without seeing the keys, then we compare.
  3. Mutation tests. Approved questions are broken on purpose: wrong key, two correct options, a quote that isn't in the book, the answer leaked into the prompt. The tool must catch them.
