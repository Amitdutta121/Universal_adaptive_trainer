Adaptive Engine Architecture

How the engine picks the next question for one student and folds their answer back in — one curriculum topic at a time, per ADR-041 and ADR-047.

Fixed thresholds

The mechanism is fixed by ADR-041 and ADR-047 — these are the tunable numbers inside it, re-tuned against simulated students in tune_adaptive_params.py.

p_init
0.10 — starting BKT mastery for a topic no attempt has touched yet
LOW_MASTERY_CEILING
0.45 — below this, difficulty is easy
MEDIUM_MASTERY_CEILING
0.85 — above this, difficulty is hard
TOPIC_ADVANCE_CEILING
0.60 — above this, the topic retires and the next one becomes current
WEAKNESS_LEARNING_RATE
0.25 — how far one score moves a subtopic's weakness
MIN_SUBTOPIC_WEAKNESS
0.05 — weakness floor, so a mastered subtopic stays reachable
Source: app/adaptive/service.py (_draw, _current_topic_pool), app/adaptive/selection.py, app/adaptive/state.py, app/domain/mastery.py — decisions recorded as ADR-041 and ADR-047 in docs/DECISIONS.md.
